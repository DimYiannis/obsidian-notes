## Overview

Facebook's (now Meta's) AI research lab. Produced the original RAG paper, along with its core components — DPR (Dense Passage Retriever) and contributions building on BART.

## Key Facts

- Published *Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks* (Lewis et al., NeurIPS 2020), introducing the RAG architecture and the term "parametric vs non-parametric memory" for LLM knowledge.
